Calculate and convert, all in one place.

MetriFlow brings a scientific calculator and a unit converter together in one web app. Work through trigonometry, roots and powers, or switch kilometres to miles, kilograms to grams and litres to millilitres without leaving the page.

It works on phones, tablets and desktops, and you can pick the look that suits you from four colour themes.
